Privacy notice

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This is a starter privacy notice for the AURELIS website and client portal. The business owner must review and adapt it to the actual legal entity, contact details, hosting providers, data flows and applicable law before accepting real client information.

Before launch: Replace the placeholders below with your verified business contact email and legal business name. Obtain appropriate legal advice for your jurisdiction and intended services.

1. Information we collect

When a visitor creates an account, we collect their name, email address and a password stored as a one-way password hash. When an authenticated client submits an enquiry, we collect the selected service, business or organisation name, project description and submission time. Basic technical request information may be processed by the hosting provider.

2. How we use information

We use account and enquiry information to create and secure client workspaces, respond to project enquiries, assess scope, communicate about requested services, maintain business records and protect the platform.

3. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by the hosting provider, database provider and transactional email provider configured by the operator. We do not sell client enquiry information. Do not submit bank login credentials, payment-card details, passwords, government identity numbers or unnecessary sensitive personal information through the enquiry form.

4. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to manage enquiries, provide services, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Access controls, password hashing, HTTPS and database protections are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. The operator must configure backups, access reviews, retention and deletion procedures.
